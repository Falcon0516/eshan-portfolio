AXIOM: The Vault Gauntlet (Eshan H)
Run: double-click index.html (Chrome or Safari). Works offline.
Keys: Arrows/Space/PgUp/PgDn move, P Explore, R Reality Check, T notes, M sound, F fullscreen, Esc back.
Enter replays the scene beat. Deep link: index.html#scene-3
Files: data/facts.js holds all on-screen text and numbers.
js/main.js is the app shell, scenes, overlays and Explore mode.
js/sim/flow.js is the lane flow engine (bots, coins, gate flashes).
css/style.css holds the theme. assets/ holds the supplied art.
Explore mode: The Gauntlet with 8 presets and a burst limit knob.
All animated processes are labelled SIMULATION.
