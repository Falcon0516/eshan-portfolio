KAVACH, a living comic book. Author: Eshan H.
Run: double-click index.html (Chrome or Safari). Fully offline, no build step.
Keys: arrows / Space / PgUp / PgDn move, Enter replays the scene beat, P Present or Explore, R Reality Check,
T presenter notes, M sound (muted by default), F fullscreen, Esc closes overlays, then goes back to the arcade.
Deep links: index.html#scene-3 .  Add ?from=hub for the cover-flip entry.
Files: data/facts.js holds ALL on-screen text, numbers, notes and tooltips (edit here).
js/stage.js camera and world, js/scenes/scenes.js the seven scenes, js/panels.js six agent canvases and runner,
js/sim/flow.js flow engine, js/explore.js six simulations, js/overlays.js tooltips/R/T, js/main.js keys and loop.
Everything animated is a SIMULATION. Durations are relative and illustrative, never measured.
